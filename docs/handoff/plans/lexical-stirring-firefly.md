# Plan: a chat-first application ("describe it, I'll design, test and simulate it")

## 1. Context
The Goal tab was built for an older idea: a form page with a result card. You now want the product to be a **chat**: you describe a job, and the bot builds small live cards for everything it does (what it understood, the design/parts, the simulation, the test cases, the results, the build sheet) that fill in while it works, Manus-style, with the chat as the foreground.

How this plan was made: three explorers read the front end, the backend and current agent-UI patterns (Manus, ChatGPT Canvas, Claude Artifacts, v0, Devin); three designers independently designed (a Manus split workspace, a single-column chat with inline cards, a project board); three judges (first-time user, the engineer who must build it here, visual craft) scored them. The single-column chat ("Thread") won (8 / 6.5 / 7 = 21.5 against 20.2 and 18.8) and took the best ideas of the other two.

**Your decisions:** the AI plans automatically when you type a new job; this round builds the **core chat** only.

## 2. Decisions that fix what was illogical in the first idea
1. **Chat does not call the AI per message.** The free model gives ~50 requests/day and takes 2-3 minutes each. A deterministic router handles buttons, chips, `/commands` and plain edits ("make the waves bigger", "add wind 12 m/s", "test it", "build sheet"). The AI is used for **a new job** (automatic, as you chose) and for an explicit "Ask AI to revise". A visible counter shows requests left.
2. **"Everything fills in at once" is real, not faked.** The first message produces an instant offline draft from the code that already exists (job match, design search, evaluator, hazard catalogue, build sheet) and the cards fill in as each finishes, with real elapsed times. The AI plan runs beside it and arrives as a **Draft vs AI** comparison ("Use AI version / Keep mine / Use AI, keep my edits"); your edits are an ordered list replayed on top of whichever base you keep, so nothing is overwritten during the 3-minute wait. For a job no ready-made job matches, the AI plan is the first plan and the planning card shows live phases and a Stop.
3. **Test cases are first-class data**, derived from the plan's stress section and `synthesize()`, so the existing sweep engine, report writer and honesty rules stay the single source of truth. You can list, add, edit levels, remove, run one or all, and undo, by chatting or from a table. Anything the simulator cannot test appears as a hatched "Can't be tested here" row, never green, and feeds the Limits strip.
4. **One live 3D view only** (the sim runner is global, browsers allow ~16 WebGL contexts and ~6 connections). It sits inline in the newest "Watch it work" card, shrinks to a thumbnail in the dock when scrolled away, has a full-screen Focus mode, and older runs freeze into a poster with "Replaced by a newer run".
5. **Honesty is structural.** A Limits strip is pinned above the composer on every screen ("Simulation only. Simple built-in autopilot. N things not tested."), turns amber when a shown result is stale, and opens "What I can't test". A wording module plus a banned-word test (`safe`, `guarantee`, `proven`, `certified`) covers every assistant sentence.
6. **Hardware stays out of the chat.** The Parts card shows the build sheet and one link, "Drive the real rover (leaves this chat)", enabled only for the ESP32 rover. The chat page registers no global key handler and has no arm/drive/checklist action.
7. **The old UI is untouched** and stays reachable as `/workbench` content (served from today's `index.html`); the chat is a **separate page `/chat`** that loads only the scripts it needs. This avoids the old page's crash paths (`setTab`, always-on sockets, the global WASD handler) and the shared-JS-scope collisions. Making `/` open the chat is a one-line switch after you have reviewed it.
8. **Plain words everywhere** ("Watch it work", "Design", "Tests", "tough conditions", "tries", "simple built-in autopilot"); internal names stay in code and in the advanced editors.

## 3. The experience
- **Empty state:** centred column (max 720 px): headline "What do you want to build and test?", one line saying what it does and doesn't, the composer, and three tap-to-run example chips tagged *instant* (flood boat, 30 cm sewer pipe rover, field-watching drone) plus "I have my own parts". Placeholder: "e.g. a boat that reaches a person 150 m away in 1 m waves".
- **After the first message** (one column, max 760 px, 52 px top bar): the stream shows at most **4 collapsed one-row cards** (What I understood, Design, Watch it work, Tests); Results, Parts & build sheet, Improve with AI, What I can't test, Answer and "Can't do that here / Waiting" appear when relevant. Wide or editable content (test table, results matrix, full design, build sheet) opens in a **Details sheet** that slides over the right 60% (full-screen on phones). A **dock** above the composer shows the step tracker ("Tests: condition 9 of 23"), the live thumbnail, Stop and the AI timer. Every action also exists as a chip; every interpreted sentence is echoed as "I read this as... Undo".
- **Look:** dark and light themes (paired tokens, system default, manual override), system font stack (offline), 15 px prose, cards with icon + status + one-line result + chevron, every state has an icon and a word (not colour alone), reduced-motion and forced-colours fallbacks, keyboard and screen-reader friendly, mobile at 375 px.
- **Example (flood boat, offline draft):** tap the chip → in under a second "What I understood" (water, person 100 m out, waves 0.5 m; editable chips), Design (amber: "Fits 4 of 5 in the estimate: freeboard 0.18 m, needs 0.25 m", see-through snapshot), the boat on animated sea running calm ("a demonstration, not a test"), Tests (6 conditions + 3 failures, hatched "Can't be tested here: rain, fog"). You say "make the waves bigger" → "Waves top level 0.6 to 0.9 m, the simulator's maximum. Undo". You say "run all" → the Results matrix fills cell by cell; Stop works after the current condition; the answer ends with what was and was not tested and the next click ("Fix the weakest", "Make it tougher", "Get the build sheet").

## 4. What gets built (files)
**Backend (new package `simlab/chat/`, new router):**
- `simlab/dashboard/chat_router.py`: `POST /api/chat/{id}/send` (202), `GET /api/chat/{id}/events?after=seq` (Server-Sent Events + 1 s polling fallback), `GET /api/chat/capabilities`, per-card actions (machine, cases ops/run, watch, sheet, improve/apply/stop). Included in `sewerbot/dashboard/app.py` **before** the catch-all `/api/{kind}/...` and `/ws/{kind}` routes. Live telemetry keeps using the existing `/ws/lab`.
- `simlab/chat/words.py` (all assistant text, honesty-tested), `intents.py` (layers: structured actions → `/commands` → edit grammar built from `menu.load()` ids and synonyms with unit regex, relative words, clamps echoed → verbs → questions → new-job detection), `cases.py` (TestCase data + operations with 20-deep undo and stale tracking), `orchestrator.py` (turn handling, draft pipeline), `resources.py` (lanes: plan, sweep, live view; a 409 becomes a queued "Up next" chip), `budget.py` (AI requests left, cached 60 s), `store.py` (current chat state saved to `runs/chats/<id>/` so a reload restores it; atomic writes; plans re-seeded into `goal_router.PLANS`; vanished jobs shown as "Interrupted - Retry").
- Small changes to existing code: `simlab/llm/planner.py` gets `finish_from_plan(raw_plan, owned)` (the post-model half of `run_plan`, call counts unchanged); `simlab/hazards/sweep.py` gets `run_sweep(on_result=None, per_seed=False, subset=None)` (defaults return exactly today's dict); `simlab/dashboard/router.py` unchanged in this round (watching a fault or debris test is shown as "Not watchable yet", and "Watch this test" is labelled a similar run, not the exact failed try).
- Imports use `importlib`/from-imports (the package attribute `goal_router` is the router object, not the module).

**Front end (all flat in `simlab/dashboard/static/` so the existing duplicate-name audit scans them):** `chat.html`, `chat.css` (tokens prefixed `--ch-`), `chat-core.js` (the single column-0 `const SimChat`: SSE client, store, composer, card registry, action sender), `chat-cards.js`, `chat-sheet.js`, `views.js` (pure HTML builders for facts, checks, matrix, parts), everything else inside IIFEs. `viewer3d.js` gets **additive** options (`interactive`, `shadows`, `maxDpr`, `paused`), an IntersectionObserver that pauses the render loop off-screen, wheel/touch capture only when interactive, and `forceContextLoss()` on dispose. Reused as-is: `Viewer3D`, `SimEnvironment`, `SimModels`, the see-through preview, `esc`-style helpers (re-declared privately in the chat IIFE, not imported from `app.js`).

## 5. Milestones (each ships alone; full suite green at every gate; commit per milestone)
- **C0 Measure first (no UI):** time `design.propose`, `evaluate`, `preview` alone and while a Lab run holds its GL context; spike create/dispose of 20 viewers and canvas re-parenting; decide inline-thumbnail vs snapshot-only fallback; set UI copy from real numbers.
- **C1 Backend core, offline, fully pytest-covered:** store, intents (60-sentence table), cases + ops, resources, budget, router/SSE, `finish_from_plan` parity test, `run_sweep(on_result)`.
- **C2 Shell + instant draft:** empty state, composer, stream, dock, Limits strip, themes; cards: understanding, design (with snapshot), tests (read-only), parts & build sheet.
- **C3 Watch it work:** live-view lane, server-side vehicle/task/conditions resolution, inline viewer, dock thumbnail, Focus mode, camera, conditions sliders, auto calm run once.
- **C4 Editable tests + results:** edit by chat and table, stale propagation, run one/all, Results matrix filling per condition, Stop after the current condition, "Watch the weakest", "Fix the weakest", "Make it tougher".
- **C5 AI step:** automatic plan for a new job (key saved; counter visible; Stop; offline fallback card), Draft vs AI diff with touched-field protection, "Ask AI to revise".
- **C6 Polish + docs:** mobile 375 px, reduced motion, forced colours, a11y pass, honesty test, update `docs/PLANNER.md`, `docs/GOAL.md`, the judges' brief (via `tools/build_brief.py`). Deferred to a second round: training card, chat history list, saved test suites, whole-chat export, compare runs, switching `/` to the chat.

## 6. Risks and controls
- **Honesty drift** (a polished chat reads as "your design is good"): wording module + banned-word test, NOT TESTED as a first-class state, persistent Limits strip.
- **AI quota/latency:** AI only for new jobs and explicit revisions; counter; budget guard; failures fall back to the offline draft with a plain message.
- **Single live view:** the card detects a scene change and freezes; sweeps run beside it without needing the viewer.
- **Canvas re-parenting is the riskiest UI mechanism:** spiked in C0 with a designed fallback (snapshot cards + Focus mode).
- **Watch fidelity:** faults/debris are dropped by `_clean_hazards`; "Watch this test" is labelled a similar run until a test proves seed equivalence.
- **Router brittleness:** closed vocabulary from the menu, "Did you mean" chips, echo + Undo for every interpreted edit.
- **Global JS scope:** one `const SimChat`, IIFEs, extend the audit's file list.
- **Route shadowing:** route-order test for `/chat`, `/api/chat/*`.
- **Refactoring `run_plan`:** the existing fake-OpenRouter tests must stay identical.

2. Browser pane at 1440x900, 1024x768 and 375x812 in dark and light: empty state, each of the three example chips, the first draft with real elapsed times, edit-by-chat with Undo, run all with Stop, Focus mode, scroll away and the dock thumbnail, an unsupported job, the AI plan live with your saved key (watch the counter), reload mid-run.
3. Webots/hardware tools unaffected (`/workbench` pages and tests unchanged).

## 8. Honest limits
Round 1 does not include training, saved chat history, saved suites, whole-chat export or compare-runs. The offline draft only understands the three ready-made jobs; everything else needs the AI step. Results come from a simple built-in autopilot in a simulator and say so on screen.
