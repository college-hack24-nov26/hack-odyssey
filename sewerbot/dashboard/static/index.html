Before you drive locked

The USB rover is a real machine. Controls stay locked until every box is ticked; Stop always works. The rover stops by itself within 1 s if this program stops talking to it.

Rover & pipe setup

Camera tip: mount the ESP32-CAM tilted up ~10°. From a low rover a level camera sees mostly floor.
